About Me

From Technical VA To Systems Architect

Seven years ago I started as a technical virtual assistant, handling the tasks other people didn't want to do. Somewhere along the way, I stopped asking "what task needs doing" and started asking "what system would make this task disappear entirely."

That shift is what led me into automation, AI, and CRM architecture. I've spent the years since building funnels, email systems, and GoHighLevel workflows for 32+ e-commerce brands, health & wellness businesses, real estate teams, and personal brands — with a best-result email campaign that returned 128% ROI in two months.

I'm a continuous learner by necessity — the tools change fast, and staying ahead of AI and automation is part of the job, not separate from it. Every certification, every new integration I pick up, feeds directly back into the systems I build for clients.

I don't measure my work in hours logged. I measure it in hours given back to the businesses I work with.
